# T066 — Sandpile and avalanche processes

Family: **Agents swarms and ecosystems**. Reviewed 24 November 2026. [Technique index](README.md).

## Mechanism

Accumulate local quantities and redistribute them when a specified threshold is exceeded.

## Proposed symbolic-music use

Use avalanches as candidates for short fills or orchestrational cascades, bounded by a phrase event budget.

## Worked example or experiment setup

Adding one token can trigger several topplings; map only a summarized avalanche size and path to one bounded musical gesture.

## Time, emotion and energy

Energy sets a fill budget; emotion chooses gesture vocabulary; defer cascades to eligible musical slots.

## Failure modes and practical limits

Unbounded avalanches can exceed deadlines. A finite simulation does not automatically establish self-organized criticality.

## Evaluation

Record size and duration distributions; compare against directly sampled fill sizes before claiming a benefit.

## AudioBus integration

Use a shared coordinator to cap activity and preserve foreground roles. Simulation interaction is not automatically musical cooperation.

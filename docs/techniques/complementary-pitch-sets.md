# T104 — Complementary pitch collections

Family: **Pitch-set geometry and harmonic spaces**. Reviewed 24 November 2026. [Technique index](README.md).

## Mechanism

Select the pitch classes absent from a collection within a declared universe.

## Proposed symbolic-music use

Use a complement as contrast material or a mutually exclusive answering vocabulary, then plan the join back.

## Worked example or experiment setup

Within twelve classes, complement is mask XOR 4095. A seven-note collection has a five-note complement.

## Time, emotion and energy

Emotion chooses when contrast is appropriate; energy remains independent; time reserves a route back to the theme.

## Failure modes and practical limits

The complement can sharply change harmonic meaning. It is not an automatic emotional opposite or consonant partner.

## Evaluation

Check double complementation and cardinality sums; audition prepared versus abrupt complement transitions.

## AudioBus integration

This family assumes twelve-tone equal temperament and octave equivalence. Preserve root, spelling, role and voicing outside the collection representation.

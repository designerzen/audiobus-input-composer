# T067 — Diffusion-limited aggregation

Family: **Agents swarms and ecosystems**. Reviewed 24 November 2026. [Technique index](README.md).

## Mechanism

Random walkers attach to an existing cluster under a declared contact rule.

## Proposed symbolic-music use

Use a growing graph as a motif genealogy or source of branching phrase routes.

## Worked example or experiment setup

Each attached branch inherits its parent motif and adds one small transformation; sample one path for a phrase.

## Time, emotion and energy

Emotion selects transformation vocabulary; energy sets active branches; growth steps are separate from beat time.

## Failure modes and practical limits

Large trees cost memory and can have unhelpful long branches. Growth geometry does not by itself create audible genealogy.

## Evaluation

Verify bounded growth and provenance, then test whether listeners recognize parent-child motif relationships.
